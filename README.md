# bike-share-user-trend-analysis
**Business Problem**
Annual members are more profitable than casual riders, so Cyclistic needs to pivot their marketing strategy from relying on building a general awareness and appealing to broad consumer segments to a focused strategy that incentivizes casual riders to convert to annual membership.
The analysis compares differences between how casual riders and annual members utilize Cyclistic bicycles to design a marketing strategy that focuses on converting casual riders into annual members.

**Data Source**
The data is collected from Cyclistic user records within the past 12 months (July 2025 to June 2026) from the internal database.

**Tools**
BigQuery was selected as the platform to perform SQL queries to clean and analyze the data due to tool availability and prior experience.
Data Studio (formerly Looker Studio) was selected as the visualization tool because of the seamless integration with BigQuery.

**Key Findings**
Key differences in time and location in user trends indicate that promotional campaigns should occur:
- during the weekends when casual riders are most likely to be active
- near the Navy Pier and DuSable Lake Shore Dr./Monroe St. stations
- with a special summer campaign from June to August when ridership peaks

Targeting these specific times and locations should improve conversion rates from casual riders into annual members.
